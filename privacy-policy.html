Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. Your privacy matters to us. In short: we do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

1. No account, no data collection

Fillora has no sign-up or account. We (RD Projects) do not collect, receive or store the details you enter. We run no servers that hold your information and use no analytics or tracking of our own.

2. Your documents stay on your device

Everything you create — your documents, saved details, photos and logos you add, and your coin balance — is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded by us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Photos and files

When you add a photo or logo, you pick it with Android's own photo picker, so the app only receives the image you choose and needs no access to your gallery or files. Images you export are saved to your Pictures/Fillora folder and are only shared when you choose to share them.

4. Permissions we use

Internet and network state — to load ads and to connect to Google Play for coin purchases. Google Play Billing — to let you buy coin packs. Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to show ads and measure them; you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Google › Ads). The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage, as described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

6. Purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs are bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google, never by us; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the coins can be added.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Security

Your information stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android. Use a screen lock to keep your phone and documents safe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available in the app.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: rdprojects24@gmail.com.
